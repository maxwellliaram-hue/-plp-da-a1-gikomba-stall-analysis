## Business problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales alongside rough stock information. She needs a clear view of total performance, growth between weeks, strong and weak trading days, weekend versus weekday patterns, and which products need restocking so she can focus limited cash on the best opportunities.

## How to run
Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top three findings
1. Total sales across the 14 days were KES 46,450, averaging KES 3,317.86 per day.
2. Week 2 sales were KES 24,400, a 10.66% increase from week 1's KES 22,050.
3. Weekend average sales were KES 4,900, which was 82.50% higher than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
